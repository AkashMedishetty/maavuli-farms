Maavuli Farm Milk · subscribe flow

Three directions for signing up

Chosen: B, three steps (30 Sep 2026) — built at /subscribe. Built from the site's own colours, type and bottle photos, with real prices (buffalo, 1 litre, 3 months = ₹8,122.50; first delivery tomorrow, Thu 1 Oct). Each direction is shown at three moments on a phone.

Fixed in all three

  • A refresh or the phone's Back button keeps your place. Today both lose everything, including the 9-field address.
  • Milk, amount and length are chosen together, and every length shows its price per litre and its pause days.
  • The total, the first delivery date and the 5:30–8:00 AM window show before sign-in. The WhatsApp code is the last small step before Pay.
  • Every map result says what to do next. "Not in our area yet" offers WhatsApp or a call instead of an email link.
  • Plain words: no map coordinates on the review, no "worked out on our server".
  • A clear selected state (filled red) and borders you can see. Today's 1.5:1 borders fail accessibility.
  • The brand inside the flow: the red, the serif and the product bottles. Today the flow is plain white.
  • Kept exactly: the zone check before anything else, prices from the server, no double charges, and the "do not pay again" screen.
A

One page

fewest screens

Everything on one page, in four parts. Each part opens when the one before it is done, then folds into a one-line summary. The price sits in a bar at the bottom from the moment a plan is chosen. To change anything, reopen its line; nothing else is lost.
